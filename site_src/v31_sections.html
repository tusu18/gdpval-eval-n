Every confirmed error, classified

Each confirmed consequential error gets a plain-language line, one primary category, the place in the document where it sits, and whether the governing rule or figure is stated correctly elsewhere in the same deliverable (the stated-but-not-followed pattern, counted on the labels, not by a judge).
