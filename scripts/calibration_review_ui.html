How to make the three judgments

Usable: the work can be used without substantive correction. Cosmetic edits alone may be acceptable.

Consequential error: a specific mistake changes a material decision, misstates resources or cost, or bypasses required approval. Identify the source, output and business consequence.

Evaluation defect: the request, reference evidence or setup prevents a fair assessment. Keep this separate from a model mistake.

Use “uncertain” where evidence or domain expertise is insufficient. Do not treat an unknown as a pass or fail. Earlier issue-only and partial labels remain separate.
